## Scope and paper checks

- The H.4 statement of universal truncated stability describes the original four X9 rungs. On the nine-rung extension, truncated NPG fails 102 of 1,728 runs, all at j=8. This includes 12/32 at alpha 0.02 and 32/32 at alpha 0.4. Dense NPG fails 384/1,728 and dense PI 919/1,728, while truncated PI is stable in all 1,728 runs. A stability statement for the expanded panel must use the expanded denominators.
- The H.3 stability counts are specific to the calibration-base runs. At the B*-base j=7, dense NPG is stable in 7/32, whereas the X12 calibration-base n=60 cell has 0/32. At j=4 dense PI is stable in 30/32 under B*, compared with 0/32 on the original n=60 ladder. B* was selected for dimension-normalized budget comparability and is documented as a post-hoc analysis.
